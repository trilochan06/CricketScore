```
Follow every ball without leaving what you're doing.

Cricket Live shows live scores ball by ball, with a big FOUR, SIX or WICKET moment on screen as it happens. Tap Pin score and a tiny live score floats over your other apps: WhatsApp, YouTube, email, anything. Pinch it smaller and park it in a corner. It updates every ball.

🏏 THE FULL PICTURE

✅ FREE. NO ADS. NO ACCOUNT.
```
