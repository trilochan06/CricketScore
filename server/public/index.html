    Optional · for MacBook

    Live scores in your MacBook's notch

    A tiny Mac app that puts the score around the notch — no window, no tab. It follows your favorite teams automatically, flashes alerts for wickets and milestones, and hides itself during full-screen apps, video calls and screen sharing. Free and open source.

    Coming soon macOS 14 Sonoma or later · Apple Silicon & Intel

    First launch takes 20 seconds, once

    It's a free app from an independent developer (not paid-registered with Apple), so macOS asks you to approve it the first time.
