    Questions

    Is Cricket Live free?

    Yes. No ads, no account, no subscription. Scores load straight into your browser, and the code is open source on GitHub.

    How do I keep the score on screen while I use other apps on Android?

    Open Cricket Live in Chrome, tap Pin score (or Pin your team's match), and a small floating score appears over your other apps. Pinch it to make it smaller and drag it to any corner. It updates every ball.

    Does it work on iPhone?

    Yes. In Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen to install it. Pin score uses iPhone's Picture in Picture to keep the score floating.

    Can I use it on a Windows laptop?

    Yes. In Chrome or Edge, click Install app in the top bar. Then use Pop out (or press P) to get a small always-on-top scoreboard while you work.

    How do starred teams work?

    Tap ☆ next to any team. Their matches move to the front and open first, and if you turn on Alerts you'll get a notification when they start, lose a wicket or finish.

    Will my team's match pin itself automatically?

    Browsers need a tap before anything can float over other apps, so it can't pin itself. Once you've pinned once, it follows your team: when their match starts, the pinned score switches to it.

    How quick are the updates?

    The open match refreshes every few seconds, ball by ball, usually within a few seconds of the TV coverage. Some smaller matches only have total updates, and those are marked.

    Is there a Mac app?

    Yes. A free Mac app puts the score around the MacBook notch and in the menu bar. It hides itself during full-screen video and calls. Download it below.
