Privacy

Short version: no accounts, no tracking, no ads, no personal data.

Website

The site loads live scores from its own server. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers. Your browser remembers your last selected match and the "All matches" toggle in local storage, on your device only. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Our hosting provider (Vercel) keeps standard, short-lived request logs (such as IP address and URL) to operate the service.

Mac app (Cricket Score)

The app connects only to our score server (to fetch scores and to check once a day whether an update is available). It doesn't collect, store or send any personal information. Your preferences are stored locally on your Mac. If you choose to add your own API key for a different data provider, it's stored in your macOS Keychain and sent only to that provider.

Data source

Scores and ball-by-ball commentary are loaded by your browser or the app directly from a third-party live-score feed. Like any website request, that service can see your device's IP address. Nothing else is sent to it.
